Help

The viewfinder is black

Collodion needs camera permission. Open Settings → Collodion → Camera and turn it on. You can still import photos from your library without camera access.

My plate did not save

Collodion needs permission to add photos. Open Settings → Collodion → Photos and choose “Add Photos Only”.

The image looks too dark or too bright

Open the dials (slider icon) and move Exposure. Every plate has a different native exposure; Ambrotype is the lightest, Tintype the deepest.

Where is the depth effect?

Depth-based focus needs an iPhone with depth capture (TrueDepth front camera, or a dual/triple rear camera). On other devices Collodion uses a soft radial fall-off instead. “DEPTH” appears at the top of the viewfinder when it is active.

Remove the COLLODION mark

Settings (gear) → Maker's mark → off.

Something else

Open an issue at github.com/zhuhuihuihui/collodion-site/issues. Include your iPhone model and iOS version.
