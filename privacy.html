Privacy policy · effective with Collodion 1.4

Privacy

Collodion does not sell personal data. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking across apps or websites. The only data that leaves your iPhone is the optional usage stats below, and Google Firebase Remote Config, which downloads app settings.

Camera

Collodion asks for camera access so you can shoot plates. Frames are processed on your iPhone and are never sent anywhere.

Photos

Collodion asks for permission to add finished plates to your photo library. When you import a photo, iOS shows you a picker and Collodion only receives the single image you choose. Collodion never reads your library.

Usage stats

From version 1.2, Collodion can send usage counts, for example that a Tintype plate was developed with the rear camera, that a plate was saved or shared, or that camera access was declined. These counts use a random identifier created on your iPhone that is not linked to you, your Apple Account or any other app. They never include photos, image data, contact details or location. They are processed by TelemetryDeck (telemetrydeck.com), which hashes the identifier again before storing it, and, from version 1.4, by Google Firebase Analytics, which also receives a Firebase app-instance identifier, device model, OS version, app version and coarse location derived from the IP address. They are used only to decide what to improve, never for ads. You can turn them off at any time in Settings → Share usage stats; turning it off also deletes anything not yet sent. Nothing is sent from the China mainland App Store.

Data we hold

Only the usage stats above, if sharing is on. Your plate settings (chosen plate, dials, maker's mark toggle) are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Children

Collodion is rated 4+. It has no ads, is not directed at children, and asks for no names, contact details or location.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
