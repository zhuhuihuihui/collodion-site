Collodion

Collodion sees light the way a glass plate did in 1851. Blue-sensitive, blind to red. Lips go dark. Skin goes porcelain. Skies burn white.

Three plates

  • Tintype — warm silver on black iron. The classic.
  • Silver — a clean studio plate. Cool and sharp.
  • Ambrotype — collodion on dark glass. Milky and soft.
